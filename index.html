1 All Hazards

Understanding dangers around us and how to stay safe — “Be Aware, Be Prepared.”

What is a Hazard?

A hazard is any source, situation or thing that can cause harm, injury, damage or loss. A hazard becomes a risk when a person is exposed to it. An “All Hazards” approach means we prepare for and stay alert to every kind of danger — whether it comes from nature, from people, or from the place where we work — instead of planning for only one type of disaster.

Types of Hazards

🌊 Natural Hazards Floods, earthquakes, cyclones, drought, landslides, lightning, heat waves and forest fires.
⚠️ Man-Made Hazards Fire accidents, road accidents, gas leaks, chemical spills, electric shock, cyber crime and conflict.
🛠️ Workplace Hazards Unsafe tools, noise, heat, sharp edges, dust, germs and stress at the place of work.

The 5 Main Workplace Hazards

  • Physical — noise, heat, vibration, slippery floor, sharp or moving machines.
  • Chemical — acids, paints, solvents, cleaning liquids, harmful fumes.
  • Biological — bacteria, viruses, fungi and other germs.
  • Ergonomic — wrong posture, lifting heavy loads, repeating the same action.
  • Psychological — stress, pressure, long working hours, bullying.

Do’s and Don’ts

✅ Do’s❌ Don’ts
Wear proper safety gear (helmet, gloves, goggles, mask).Don’t touch electrical wires with wet hands.
Keep the work area clean, dry and well-lit.Don’t block emergency exits or fire equipment.
Use tools only for the purpose they are made for.Don’t run or play in the workshop or laboratory.
Report every hazard to a teacher or supervisor at once.Don’t ignore a small leak, spark or cracking sound.
🛡️ Safety FIRST F — Fire safety  ·  I — Inspect the area  ·  R — Report hazards  ·  S — Safety gear  ·  T — Think before you act.
📞 Emergency Numbers (India) All-in-one Emergency: 112  ·  Fire: 101  ·  Ambulance: 108  ·  Police: 100

What I Learned

Most accidents do not “just happen” — they are usually the result of an ignored hazard. If we learn to spot, avoid and report hazards, we can protect ourselves and the people around us. This project taught me that safety is not a rule to fear, but a habit to build every single day.

2 Value Chain Poster

How a product or service moves from raw material to the happy customer — adding value at every step.

What is a Value Chain?

A value chain is the complete series of activities that a business performs to create a product or service and deliver it to the customer. Each step adds value — it makes the product better, faster, cheaper or more useful. The idea was given by Michael Porter in 1985.

Example: Value Chain of a Packet of Biscuits

  1. Inbound: Wheat, sugar and oil are bought and stored.
  2. Operations: Dough is mixed, shaped, baked and packed.
  3. Outbound: Packets are boxed and sent to shops.
  4. Marketing: Ads on TV and offers attract buyers.
  5. Service: Customer care handles complaints and returns.

Why is the Value Chain Important?

✔ Adds valueEvery step improves the product or service.
💸 Reduces costHelps find and remove wasteful steps.
⭐ Improves qualityFocus on what the customer really wants.
😊 Builds trustSatisfied customers come back again.
💡 Key Idea The value chain turns raw material into value. If any single link is weak, the whole chain becomes slow or costly — just like a real chain.

3 Personal Life & Service

Building a better “me” — and using my skills to serve others.

My Personal Life

My personal life is everything that shapes the person I am — my health, my habits, my goals, my values and my relationships. Managing it well means looking after my body, my mind and my time.

🌱PhysicalExercise, hygiene, healthy food, enough sleep.
🧠MentalReading, learning new skills, positive thinking.
❤️SocialRespecting elders, teamwork, good manners.

Good Personal Habits I Practice

  • Waking up on time and following a daily routine.
  • Keeping myself, my books and my room neat and clean.
  • Being honest, punctual and polite in my speech.
  • Managing my time between study, play and rest.
  • Setting small goals and working to achieve them.

Service — Helping Others

Service means using our time, effort and skills to help others without expecting anything in return. Service makes our family, school, and society better, and it also builds our own character.

🏫 At SchoolKeeping classrooms clean, helping classmates, taking part in drives and events.
🏠 At HomeHelping parents, caring for younger siblings and elders, sharing household work.
🌳 In SocietyTree plantation, cleanliness drives, helping neighbours, spreading awareness.
🇮🇳 For the NationFollowing rules, saving water and electricity, being a responsible citizen.
